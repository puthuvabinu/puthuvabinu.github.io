People sit at the centre of every decision. That perspective shapes design thinking, collaboration with teams, and relationships with clients. Curiosity plays a big role in the process. Questions are explored from different angles, with an effort to leave no stone unturned. MY STORY

"I have no special talent. I am only passionately curious." Albert Einstein

That line has stayed with me for a long time.

Growing up in India meant being surrounded by colour and visual energy everywhere. One of my earliest memories is when we got our first colour television at home. Watching cartoons in colour for the first time felt magical. Around the same time, I found myself drawn to advertisements, especially the visual effects, even though I had no idea how any of it was made.

Years later, during a casual conversation, I asked a friend how visual effects were created. He confidently told me it was all done using something called Java. It turned out to be completely wrong, but that moment stuck with me. It sparked a curiosity that didn’t really go away. It took me years to realise Java is a programming language, not a visual effects tool, but by then I was already heading in that direction.

That curiosity led me to study Media Convergence and Visual Effects, and eventually into the world of design.

I started my career at Assyst in Kochi, working on web design, graphics, 3D animation, and user interfaces. Over time, my focus began to shift. I became more interested in how things worked for people, not just how they looked. That naturally led me into user experience.

At EY, things started to come together. It was the right environment to go deeper into UX, refine my thinking, and build a stronger foundation. That’s where I also completed my HFI-CUA™ certification. After a few years, I felt ready to take on more responsibility and moved into a role at QBurst Technologies, leading a UX team.

At a turning point in my life, I made the decision to move to New Zealand and start a new chapter. It was a big shift, both personally and professionally, but also an exciting opportunity to grow and explore new perspectives.

Today, I’m based in Wellington, working as a UX designer at Totara Learning. The journey has taken me across different roles, teams, and places, but at its core, it has always been driven by curiosity, creativity, and a genuine love for design.

And that’s still what drives me today.

PROCESS I FOLLOW

Classics are always crafted with a great passion, a pinch of craziness, and with an immense method. I follow a method that turns your ideas into marvelous products.

Discover

In the first step, it is all about gaining understanding and knowledge. To craft a better solution, I dig deep to discover everything possible.

Business

I will begin with understanding the company's vision and project goals.

Market

Product's usefulness is mapped along with its competitors.

Users

Understanding user's requirements and their behavior always does the trick!

Define

I pull insights from the research data and craft a product roadmap that solves problems.


Workshop

Validate the key performance indicators for users and the company.

Information Architecture

Organize the content intuitively to guide users in completing the desired action seamlessly.

User Journey

I will arrive at a design that makes an experience genuinely pleasing for the users.

Design

It's all about bringing emotions to logic. I map empathy and cognitive science to derive on a product that is not just usable but appealing.

Wireframes

I will simplify the story that particularly focuses on the prioritization of content, functionalities, and behaviors.

Visual Design

I give life to designs by adding visually appealing elements that evoke emotions.

Prototyping

I always test the product by building a usable mockup to get primary feedback.
