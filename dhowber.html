Brief

The Nau app is a marketplace that brings together boat captains (nukhdas) and traders who want to ship their cargo to nearby ports. Nukhdas can define the dhow route, show the boat's capacity and connect with traders. Both can register, search, check availability and book cargo shipments, all from one easy-to-use mobile app.

Payments are handled in the app, so nukhdas are paid immediately. A two-sided rating system lets traders and nukhdas rate each other, and traders can track their cargo from departure to arrival.

The Nau app is overseen by the Dubai Future Foundation (DFF) and is part of the Dubai 10X initiative.

About 1,500 dhows make 13,000 trips a year, carrying around 2.2 million tonnes of cargo worth Dh22 million. The platform can scale and be replicated around the world, especially in countries with large inland waterways, rivers and harbours.
